## The problem

Antibody discovery generates far more candidates than can be characterised
experimentally. The bottleneck is not finding binders; it is deciding which of them
are worth the assay time, early enough for the decision to matter.

## What was built

AI and simulation-driven workflows for antibody selection and engineering, running
over lab-automation data, plus the data architecture and automated pipelines
underneath them.

## What it changed

Deployed across more than thirty clinical targets.
