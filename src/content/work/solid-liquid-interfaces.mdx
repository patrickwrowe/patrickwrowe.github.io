## The problem

How water behaves against a surface governs friction, flow and nucleation at the
nanoscale, and it is exactly the regime where continuum models stop applying and
first-principles methods become unaffordable. Two-dimensional materials make the
problem sharper: the interface is essentially all there is.

## What was built

A machine-learned framework for constructing interface potentials, applied to water
on two-dimensional materials.

## What it changed

Carried out with experimentalists at the National Graphene Institute.
