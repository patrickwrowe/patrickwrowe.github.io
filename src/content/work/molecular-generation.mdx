## The problem

Generating valid SMILES is harder than the papers make it sound. A model that
produces syntactically correct strings most of the time still produces a stream of
molecules that a chemist would not consider, and the failure modes are not the ones
you would predict from validation loss.

## What was built

An autoregressive transformer over SMILES, trained from scratch rather than
fine-tuned, with sampling and property filtering.

## What it changed

The sampling loop runs client-side. There is no server, no cold start and no cost per
request.

## How to check it

The demo will let you sample from the model directly and see the validity and
property statistics of what comes out, including where it fails.
