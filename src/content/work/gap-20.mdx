## The problem

Carbon is awkward. The same element gives graphite, diamond, nanotubes and a
continuum of amorphous structures, and the bonding differs enough between them that
an empirical potential fitted to one phase tends to be wrong about the others.
Density functional theory gets it right and is far too slow for the system sizes and
timescales that matter.

## What was built

A Gaussian approximation potential trained on DFT reference data, covering graphite,
diamond, the amorphous phases and surfaces in a single model rather than a set of
phase-specific fits.

## What it changed

Adopted in academia and industry for structure search, phase-diagram prediction and
graphitisation studies.
